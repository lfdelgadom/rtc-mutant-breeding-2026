MUTANT PIPELINE PRACTICAL (RTC RER5030, Topic 10)

1. Double click Mutant_pipeline.Rproj (opens RStudio or Positron).
2. Run install_packages.R once (needs internet).
3. Open Mutant_pipeline.Rmd. Change CROP at the top
   ("chrysanthemum", "wheat" or "bean"), then Knit or run chunk by chunk.
4. Answer the 13 questions inside the file.
   The single trial step takes a few minutes (SpATS in every trial and trait).

Files
  helpers_mutant.R   the functions of the pipeline (free packages only: ggplot2, lme4, SpATS)
  data/              simulated trials of three crops
